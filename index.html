7-Day Andhra Bulking Plan

Cook on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Non-veg only at lunch, veg dinners.

Your daily meals

Estimates are rough. They aim for about 2,600–2,800 kcal, 125+ g protein and 30+ g fiber a day, which assumes a body weight of about 70–75 kg. Rice is cooked fresh every day, 1–1.5 cups cooked. Chicken or salmon portions are 150–200 g. Eggs count as non-veg, so they are left out. Calcium and iron come from curd, almond milk, dal and chana, vitamin C from tomato, salad and fruit, and omega-3 from chia, flax and salmon. Vitamin D and B12 are likely gaps with veg dinners, so ask your doctor about a blood test before taking supplements. If fiber at this level bloats you in week 1, cut the roasted chana by half and increase it slowly.
